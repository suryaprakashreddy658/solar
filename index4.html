Solar Subsidy – Telangana

  • Residential rooftop systems are eligible for government subsidy.
  • Up to 3 kW → 40% subsidy.
  • 3–10 kW → 20% subsidy on additional capacity.
  • Net-metering reduces monthly electricity bills further.

Subsidy availability depends on MNRE & state policy at the time of installation.
